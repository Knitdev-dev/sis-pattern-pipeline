Selvedge Stitches. All stitch counts in this pattern include 2 selvedge stitches — one at each edge. On RS rows: slip the first stitch, knit the last stitch. On WS rows: slip the first stitch, purl the last stitch. These stitches are not mentioned explicitly in the instructions; work them automatically at the start and end of every row.
Cable panel. This front is worked with a central sand-cable panel of {SaCaSIS_K} cables ({SaCaSIS_Panel_sts} stitches), flanked by {SaCaSIS_Flank_sts_each} stockinette stitches on each side. The panel pulls in approximately 15 % horizontally relative to stockinette; the body cast-on already accounts for this. Refer to the SaCaSIS chart on p. {Sacasis_chart_page} for the 10-row repeat.
