6. Assembly & Finishing

With all four pieces complete, it's time to join them. Work through the steps below in order.

1. Block Before Seaming (Optional)

Block all pieces flat to their finished measurements and allow to dry fully before seaming. This is optional but highly recommended — it evens out the fabric and makes seaming much easier.

2. Join Shoulder Seams

With right sides facing outward, join the front and back at both shoulders using mattress stitch. Match bound-off stitches edge to edge. Work from the armhole edge inward. Video tutorial.

3. Set In the Sleeves

Align the centre of the sleeve cap to the shoulder seam, and match the underarm bind-off of the sleeve to the underarm bind-off of the body. Pin in place, then ease the cap evenly into the armhole. Sew with mattress stitch, distributing any fullness smoothly around the cap.

4. Sew Side Seams

With right sides facing outward, join the front and back at the sides using mattress stitch, working from the hem upward to the underarm. Written tutorial.

5. Sew Sleeve Seams

Join each sleeve seam using mattress stitch, working from the cuff upward and joining to the underarm seam at the top.

6. Final touch

Weave in all ends.
