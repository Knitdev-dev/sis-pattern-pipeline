4. Back

Cast On and Ribbed Band

Cast on {Back_width_st} sts using smaller needles. Work the 1×1 ribbed band as follows. RS: k1, p1 to end. WS: if the stitch count is even, k1, p1 to end; if uneven, p1, k1 to end. Continue until the ribbed band measures 9 cm. Then change to larger needles.

After ribbed band Sts on needle: {Back_width_st}. Band measures 9 cm.

Body to Armhole

RS: Knit to end.
WS: Purl to end.

Work until piece measures {Body_length_to_armhole_cm} cm from cast-on edge. End after a WS row.

After body Rows: {PP11}. Sts: {Back_width_st} — unchanged.

Underarm Bind Off

Bind off {Underarm_bindoff_sts} sts at the beginning of the next 2 rows. {Sts_after_UA_bindoff} sts remain.

After underarm bind-off Rows: {PP11_plus_2}. Sts: {Sts_after_UA_bindoff}.

Armhole Decreases

Decrease 1 st at each end of every RS row to shape both armhole edges.

RS (decrease row): k1, ssk, knit to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1.
WS: Purl to end.

Work {Armhole_decrease_rows} RS decrease rows in total. Finish with a WS row. {Shoulder_width_st} sts remain.

After armhole decreases Rows: {PP11_plus_2_plus_arm}. Sts: {Shoulder_width_st}. Check: {Armhole_decrease_sts} sts decreased in total — {Armhole_decrease_rows} per side.

Work Even

RS: Knit to end.
WS: Purl to end.

Work {PP22} rows. End after a WS row.

After working even Rows: {PP11_plus_2_plus_arm_plus_PP22}. Sts: {Shoulder_width_st} — unchanged.

Back Neck and Shoulder Shaping

The back neck is shallow (2.5 cm). The shoulders are bound off at the same time as the back neck is shaped.

RS (division row): Knit {Sts_to_base_of_back_neck} sts, place the central {PP21} sts on a holder, place the remaining stitches on a second holder. The live stitches are the right back.

Tip: When transferring the held left back stitches back to the needle, take a moment to check that they are all sitting with the right leg at the front of the needle. Twisted stitches at the shoulder join are easy to introduce here and harder to spot later.
WS (row 1): Purl to end.
RS (row 2): Knit to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1.
WS (row 3): Purl to end.
RS (row 4): Bind off {Bindoff_group_1} sts, knit to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1.
WS (row 5): Purl to end.
RS (row 6): Bind off {Bindoff_group_2} sts.
WS (row 7): Purl to end.
RS: Bind off all remaining stitches.

Transfer the held left back stitches to the needle and work to match, reversing all shaping (neck decrease becomes ssk; bind-offs at beginning of RS rows).

Back piece complete Both shoulders bound off. Back neck: {PP21} sts on holder.
