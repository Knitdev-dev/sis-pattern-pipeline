1. Pattern Details

About This Pattern

This sweater is worked flat in stockinette stitch, from the bottom up, in four separate pieces: a front, a back, and two sleeves. Once all pieces are complete, they are seamed together at the shoulders, sides, and underarms using mattress stitch, with the sleeves set in last. The neckband is then picked up and worked in the round on smaller needles.

A Note on Gauge

Getting the fit right depends on gauge — and gauge can be surprisingly unpredictable. Even if your swatch matched, the fabric of a finished sweater often behaves differently: gravity, seaming, and the sheer volume of the piece can all shift things slightly. Ideally, gauge is measured over a larger area on a garment that has already been knitted, blocked, and worn. That's not always practical, of course — but it's worth keeping in mind.
