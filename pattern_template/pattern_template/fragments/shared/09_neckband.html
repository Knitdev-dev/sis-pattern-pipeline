7. Neckband

V-Neck Band

Pick up and knit the stitches around the V-neck opening as follows.

Refer to the schematic. Starting from the right back corner (marked with a star), pick up with smaller circular needles:

  • {PP21} stitches from the centre back, which you placed on a holder,
  • 5 stitches from the left back corner shaping,
  • 3 stitches for every 4 rows along the left front straight section,
  • 4 stitches for every 5 rows along the left V diagonal,
  • slip the 1 held centre stitch from its holder onto the right needle (this becomes the central stitch of the mitred decrease),
  • 4 stitches for every 5 rows along the right V diagonal,
  • 3 stitches for every 4 rows along the right front straight section,
  • 5 stitches from the right back corner shaping,
  • then join to work in the round.

Work in k1, p1 rib, ensuring there is a knit stitch at the base of the V — this is your central stitch. On every round, work a mitred decrease on either side of the central stitch (2 stitches decreased per round), as follows.

Round 1: Work in rib to 1 stitch before the central stitch. You will have a purl, a knit, and a purl on the left needle. Work the mitred decrease (video tutorial). Continue in rib to end.
Round 2: As Round 1, except the 3 stitches before the decrease will be three knits rather than purl-knit-purl. Work the mitred decrease in the same way.

Repeat these two rounds throughout.

Optional stripe: After the band measures 1.5 cm, switch to a contrasting yarn for 2 rounds, then return to the main yarn. A small detail, but a nice one.

Continue until the neckband measures 3 cm. To finish, work 2 rounds of double knitting to prepare for the Italian bind-off: Round 1: *k1, sl1 purl-wise wyif; repeat from * to end. Round 2: *sl1 wyib, p1; repeat from * to end. Then work the Italian bind-off, still working the mitred decrease at the central stitch on the bind-off round. ⚠ Flag: no Italian bind-off video was found in the existing file — please add a link if available.
