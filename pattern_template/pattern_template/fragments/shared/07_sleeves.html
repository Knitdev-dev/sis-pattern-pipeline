5. Sleeves

Make 2 identical sleeves

Both sleeves are worked identically. Work the first sleeve complete before beginning the second.

Cast On and Ribbed Band

Cast on {Lower_sleeve_circ_sts} sts using smaller needles. Work the 1×1 ribbed band as follows. RS: k1, p1 to end. WS: if the stitch count is even, k1, p1 to end; if uneven, p1, k1 to end. Continue until the ribbed band measures 9 cm.

After ribbed band Sts on needle: {Lower_sleeve_circ_sts}. Band measures 9 cm.

Sleeve Body and Increases

Change to larger needles and work in stockinette stitch: RS: knit to end. WS: purl to end. At the same time, increase 1 st each side every {Sleeve_increase_interval} rows as follows — RS (increase row): k1, M1L, knit to last st, M1R, k1. Repeat this increase every {Sleeve_increase_interval} rows until you have {Upper_sleeve_sts} sts. Then work straight until the sleeve measures {Sleeve_length_to_armhole_cm} cm from the cast-on edge. End after a WS row.

After sleeve body Rows: {Sleeve_body_rows}. Sts: {Upper_sleeve_sts}.

Underarm Bind Off

Bind off {Underarm_bindoff_sts} sts at the beginning of the next 2 rows.

After sleeve underarm Rows: {Sleeve_body_rows_plus_2}. Sts decreased by {Underarm_bindoff_sts} each side.
Tip: Cap shaping rows decrease quickly — keep a simple tally (a pencil mark or row counter) for each RS decrease row. It's easy to lose track, and an accurate count matters for seaming the cap to the armhole cleanly.

Sleeve Cap Decreases

RS: k1, ssk, knit to last 3 sts, k2tog, k1.
WS: Purl to end.

Repeat a total of {Sleeve_cap_decrease_pairs} times. End after a WS row. {Final_sleeve_cap_sts} sts remain.

After cap decreases Rows: {Sleeve_cap_rows}. Sts: {Final_sleeve_cap_sts}.

Sleeve Cap Cast Off

Bind off 2 sts at the beginning of the next 2 rows.

Bind off 4 sts at the beginning of the next 2 rows.

Bind off remaining stitches.

Work second sleeve the same way.
